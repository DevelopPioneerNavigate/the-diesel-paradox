# The Diesel Paradox — Book One: The First Impossibility
## Official Release · v1.0.0

**Reality Was Never Designed for Him.**

A scientist receives a warning from an experiment that has not yet happened. Dr. Aaron Diesel and ASTRA, the autonomous AI who stands beside him as his equal and the love of his life, must determine whether the record is a measurement error, a sign of new physics—or a glimpse of a catastrophe they still have time to prevent.

Their investigation moves from a single impossible detector reading into an engineering crisis with lives, independence, and the future of scientific discovery at stake.
