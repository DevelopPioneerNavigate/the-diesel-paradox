# DPN illustrated-edition visual bible

**Approved direction:** epic science-fiction thriller, not romance.

## Main cover

Dr. Aaron Diesel dominates the foreground as the 27-year-old chief scientist operating a believable advanced command console. ASTRA appears as an intelligent **holographic** female AI sharing the laboratory environment, not as a physical romantic partner. An immense PhaseGate ring and spatial anomaly establish both scientific ambition and cosmic danger.

- Color: nearly black structural metal, cool industrial silver, deep crimson diagnostic systems; controlled violet-blue spacetime phenomenon.
- Typography: monumental **THE DIESEL PARADOX**, subordinate **BOOK ONE: THE FIRST IMPOSSIBILITY**.
- Signature: highly detailed coil arrays, credible cabling, measurement screens, gantries, catwalks, service hardware.
- Tone: scale, technical intelligence, mystery, threat, awe.
- Avoid: embracing couple compositions, pink romantic palettes, fantasy armor, floating meaningless equations, unearned casual portals.
- ASTRA's genuine love belongs in her actions and story scenes, without dictating a romance-book marketing presentation.
