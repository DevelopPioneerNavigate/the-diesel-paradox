# Narrative continuity and canon gates

## Immutable canon

- Book One starts with **Dr. Aaron Diesel aged 27**, founder, CEO and principal inventor at fictional DPN Technology.
- ASTRA is an autonomous female AI. **She genuinely loves Diesel and he loves her**. Her care is freely chosen; she has opinions, boundaries and agency. The novel is primarily hard-science-inspired adventure, not romance.
- Diesel is the smartest living human and finds credible solutions through deduction, engineering and planned contingencies; he never receives convenient unexplained powers or a Morty-style companion.
- Machines are long-running characters in the engineering sense: they have identifiable signatures, service histories, defects, revisions, and consequences.
- Book One PhaseGate PG-1 is **not** a walk-through portal. It is a measurement-and-field prototype that reveals anomalous boundary response.
- Established physics does not confirm time travel, traversable wormholes, free vacuum energy or entanglement-based teletransportation.
- Fictional model: **Boundary-Response Manifold (BRM)**; see series bible for R1–R6.

## Per-chapter editorial checklist

- [ ] Scene advances discovery, danger, relationships or consequences.
- [ ] The science distinguishes real knowledge from fictional premises.
- [ ] Every system used has prior setup or a clear and credible derivation.
- [ ] Diesel stays competent, witty, thoughtful, and technically hands-on.
- [ ] ASTRA remains autonomous and her love is conveyed through *actions*, not exposition.
- [ ] Resource use, maintenance, injuries, destruction, personnel, and energy accounting remain coherent.
- [ ] No "reset button" repairs earlier damage.
- [ ] Physics costs and power/cooling constraints do not disappear.
- [ ] Major scientific clues connect to the 42-chapter payoff register.
- [ ] A newly invented term is added to the bible before it spreads.

## Chapter Three locked observations

- At 4:51 p.m. after his negotiated five-hour rest, Diesel visits DPN-001; he arrives after Chapter Two's LANTERN test.
- The original L-03 quartz oscillator / notch network was found within the workbench, not stolen.
- Test found conventional damaged insulation leakage but an asymmetry-dependent residual that survived cable replacement; **it has not been demonstrated to be exotic physics**.
- QRF-17 / REV B / NOTCH FRAME appears in an old diagram; the future induction-loop schematic contains QRF-17 / RETURN FRAME. Similar identifier is *not proof* of causal identity.
- The old handwritten remark is **NOT RESONANCE. RESPONSE.**; preserve ambiguity about what he meant.
- Isolated optical monitor recorded a brief unexplained interval before the scheduled dummy-load step, but requires blind repetition and reflection controls.
- ASTRA notices an archival filename ASTRA_FIRST_CONVERSATION_RAW_02 with suspect modification chronology and flags it for in-person review. The artifact is not yet opened or authenticated.

## Chapter Four locked observations

- PG-1 is **not** returned to service in Chapter Four; Sector Six still has structural damage and the newly designed protection architecture needs qualification.
- `LITTLE ARGUMENT` is a separate **conventional** coil/protection scale test rig, not a spacetime portal. It can demonstrate current, switching, thermal, and sensor-fault behavior without recreating exotic effects.
- Revisions distinguish **HUMAN-SAFE**, **MAGNET-SAFE**, **DATA-PRESERVING**, and separately verified physical state. A command acknowledgement does not prove stored energy has been safely dissipated.
- A mislabeled differential voltage sense pair could report **zero** although a physical circuit remained energized. Controller's permissive is rejected after test.
- A forgotten maintenance bond created an unintended current path during a shielded, restricted scale test. Diesel held the ramp and balanced branch potentials using a verified separate bypass before slow discharge. No personnel entered the cage.
- Passive interlocks remain physically independent of ASTRA and supervisory software; a hardware-keyed maintenance link is now required, plus independent sensing. Safety is independent of CEO command.
- The fault exposed an inadequate work process rather than a negligent individual. Diesel accepted design ownership and protected staff.
- Future `QRF-17 / RETURN FRAME` loop drawing has a bracket clearance matching **damaged** Sector Six dimensions, which does not prove future provenance but deepens the original mystery.
- ASTRA and Diesel defer forensic analysis of the old `ASTRA_FIRST_CONVERSATION_RAW_02` archive until in-person review with proper provenance.
- Chapter Five will investigate the eleven-minute offset through blind, controlled repetitions, not assert that time travel is established.

## Chapter Five locked observations

- PG-1 remains **offline** pending Sector Six repairs and independent quench certification. Chapter Five's optical experiment does not energize the PhaseGate.
- In DPN-301, Diesel, Elena Mora, Jian Rao, ASTRA and independent analysts use **pre-registered controls**, shuffled trial types, blinded analysis, physically separated safety monitoring, independent clocks, and sealed photonic records.
- The test conditions include dark, rejected-band, active candidate, reversed modulation, electronically simulated, and shuttered sham trials. Controls reveal ordinary contamination including a thermal sensor mounting fault, stray optical reflection and amplifier reset. These are **disclosed, not hidden**.
- One physical optical pre-exposure occurs approximately **eleven minutes** before a subsequently scheduled three-interval optical pulse. A blinded first-pass comparison finds a related modulation and established event order. **It is not definitive proof** of future-to-past information transfer; schedule leakage, unknown signal coupling, and sensor faults must still be investigated.
- The full blind schedule was generated and sealed **before** the trial; this means a conventional information leak from the schedule remains a leading hypothesis. Lack of an authorized access log does not conclusively exclude leakage.
- The write-once photonic detector gives physical exposure order, independent of merely trusting network timestamps.
- Diesel commissions **WINDOW-01**, a separate low-energy replication rig with remote randomized scheduling, a second detector batch, independent optical channels and blind analysis. This is future work; **WINDOW-01 is not built yet**.
- ASTRA's distributed safety subsystem monitors hazards but does not access the experimental randomization during the blinded trial. Her genuine love for Diesel appears through care and chosen support without dictating the scientific result.
- An external electrical power-quality monitor records a small three-part transient at the same time as the optical pre-exposure. The correlation may have a **conventional common cause**; cause is unproven. A historical utility log allegedly contains a similar event from the early Origin Lab era and its provenance is **unverified**. Do not reveal the Directorate or Curator as the source here.
- Chapter Six should introduce the Continuity Directorate based on a fragmentary signature; its information should be plausible from infrastructure monitoring and **not** omniscient discovery.

## Chapter Six locked observations

- The **Continuity Directorate** is introduced through regional timing-reference and utility infrastructure anomalies. Sloane is the director and Tomas Redd her deputy; Martin Vale is the liaison. Its full mandate is *not* established. The public-facing title used is **Regional Continuity Assessment Office**.
- Directorate analysts know only **fragmentary external waveforms** and a geographic association. They do **not** have DPN optical source data, access to ASTRA, the future packet, or detailed PhaseGate designs. Do not give them omniscient knowledge in Chapter Seven.
- A regional reference relay showed a phase-reference discrepancy at approximately 12:03 and a later reduction ~11 minutes afterward. Two independent but regionally exposed reference devices are not proof of nonordinary physics: supply interference, GNSS/common-environment effects and firmware timing correction remain alternatives.
- Sloane does not adopt **CLASS IMPOSSIBLE** as a proven result. The old category merely records failures to fit an instrument model. A historical Directorate memo says **MEASURE THE CLOCKS. NOT THE STORY.** It predates Diesel but its full source/history is unrevealed.
- The historic utility waveform chart near the Origin Lab has **no original raw data or calibration record**; a photograph carries **CONFIRM SITE ISOLATION. SOURCE NOT ESTABLISHED.** The historical physical association remains unverified.
- Diesel truthfully confirms PG-1 had an earlier protection failure, has been repaired only in scale prototype design, and **remains OFFLINE**. He offers narrow external hazard and facility-demand information but neither private research data nor ASTRA's internals without a justified, legally supported safety need.
- ASTRA joins Sloane's call openly as an autonomous technical participant. Diesel explicitly respects her right to speak for herself. Mutual genuine love is woven through choices and private concern.
- Sloane and Diesel share respect for measured uncertainty and public safety despite conflicting positions on independent experimental oversight. First in-person technical meeting is **proposed**, not held.
- WINDOW-01 remains **unbuilt**: procurement and independent replication design are underway; no second definitive experiment has occurred.
- Chapter Seven: Sloane offers cooperation and quietly arranges surveillance; ASTRA detects it and blocks Diesel from an unsafe retaliation. Preserve earned technical deductions and no intrusive omniscience.

## Chapter Seven locked observations

- Diesel and Director Sloane meet in person at **a neutral engineering arbitration center in Grand Rapids**; ASTRA and Elena attend. A **limited technical working group is proposed but no agreement is yet signed**.
- Sloane's limited lawful public-metadata monitoring authorized external observation. Contractor's shared-service support inquiry requested connectivity information **outside the permitted scope**; this was neither a confirmed restricted-system intrusion nor proof of Sloane authorizing the excess.
- The shared environmental telemetry provider supports **other customers including hospital and municipal water facilities**. Diesel initially considers using an active response to trace the contractor; **ASTRA independently refuses** because unknown shared-service dependencies could affect bystanders. Diesel immediately agrees and chooses passive evidence collection and narrow permission isolation.
- Provider's preliminary review confirms the improper request was **canceled before any private DPN connectivity information was returned through it**, although investigation continues. Do not imply a proven successful breach.
- Sloane pauses related contractor work and removes broader collection authority; she demands Redd's written account and temporarily removes him from contractor coordination, but suspicion and oversight tension persist.
- No one may use a network or infrastructure service shared with civilian safety systems as an investigative testbed without clear safety and legal authority.
- A separate **failed legacy communications-session record** appears on the retired DPN-001 monitoring interface for a disconnected old number. No voice call was completed, no sender authenticated, no actual physical communication demonstrated; caller-ID/session metadata can be falsified and instrument artifacts remain candidate explanations. Three-part interval resembles other anomalies incompletely. It gives the title **The Call They Can't Make**, not a demonstrated supernatural phone call.
- The protected file `ASTRA_FIRST_CONVERSATION_RAW_02` remains **unopened**; Diesel and ASTRA reaffirm plan to examine it together with proper provenance.
- WINDOW-01 is still **not built**; components on order. PG-1 remains offline and Sector Six under repairs. No new physical BRM principle is established in this chapter.
- Chapter Eight: **The First Debt** should introduce measurable phase-drift/debt via new observations and a changed experiment sequence, not declare full BRM truth. The Directorate/Curator should not know secret DPN findings without credible evidence.

## Chapter Eight locked observations

- This chapter concludes **Act I: The Warning**. WINDOW-01 is now **assembled and initially qualified** as a *low-energy optical* apparatus in DPN-301. PG-1 PhaseGate at DPN-401 **remains OFFLINE** with Sector Six under repair and full quench safety qualification outstanding.
- WINDOW-01 uses independent optical detection, new detector fabrication batch, a third-party clock reference, physical sealed recording media, and a separate future randomization annex. Future pulse selection is physically generated late in the interval, not fully available at the beginning.
- A faint candidate **physical pre-exposure** occurs at 17:08. The protocol includes a pre-registered, separately authorized intervention/sham option. At 17:18 a branch selection diverts source energy into an absorber, so **no matching optical pulse is emitted** when the later pulse time arrives. The earlier physical record remains.
- This does **NOT** demonstrate a prevented future event, time travel, or a BRM law. Detector faults, hidden light/EM pathways, leakage of candidate patterns, and analysis selection effects remain alternatives. Blind analysis preserves candidate detection before branch decoding.
- Several *nearby* metrology instruments record a small accumulating **phase disagreement** after the intervention. Environmental vibration and thermal coupling explain part but not all of a **statistically unresolved residual**; **no external regional hazard is observed**. The phase drift relaxes under passive isolation; spatial correlation toward the recorder is exploratory.
- Diesel defines **D* (D-star)** as a *provisional integrated residual-phase metric*, nicknamed **causal debt**. It is an operational proxy and **not a proven universal conserved quantity, energy source, or physical constant**. Later experiments may refine or falsify it.
- The team suspends further active tests until the unexplained residual is understood. A future **WINDOW-02** environmental boundary and blinded replication test is **designed but not built**. DPN-501 energy/material specialists will join future work as the scale increases.
- Real-world no-signaling, energy and heat limits remain in force as scientific baseline; none of this implies free time travel or functioning traversable portals.
- The original `ASTRA_FIRST_CONVERSATION_RAW_02` archive and DPN-001 failed communications record remain unresolved and isolated. Sloane's cooperation working group remains **proposed and unsigned**.
- ASTRA chooses independent science/safety standards and cares for Diesel's physical and emotional well-being; Diesel reciprocates while making earned technical deductions. Their relationship is foundational, not the novel's marketing genre.
- Chapter Nine *Heat Has to Go Somewhere* must build from **real stored energy and cooling/transfer engineering** in DPN-501; do not claim D* has been established as directly interconvertible with energy.
